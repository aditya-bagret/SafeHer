Figure 7
Crime Danger Index Formula and Distribution
The CDI formula combines violent crime rate with log-scaled crime frequency. Converting to a percentile rank produces a perfectly uniform [0,1] distribution — exactly 30% of cells exceed 0.7. This is the primary novel contribution resolving label ambiguity.
CDI Formula
CDI(cell) = violent_rate(cell)
            × log₁(1 + crime_count(cell))
violent_rate = crimes with severity ≥ 3 ÷ total crimes in cell
crime_count = total historical incidents in cell
log-scaling prevents high-volume low-severity cells from dominating
Two-step conversion:
1. Compute CDI per cell → raw score
2. CDI.rank(pct=True) → uniform [0,1]
Exactly 30% of cells exceed 0.7 (high-risk threshold)

Figure 17
SOS Alert Flow — Testing Results
SOS emergency alert system flow and test outcomes across 50 test cases. The system triggers when a user enters a cell with combined risk > 0.75 and/or manually activates the SOS button. Alerts are dispatched to pre-configured emergency contacts via SMS and email within 2 seconds.
1
Trigger detection
Location service monitors user's current grid cell. Risk score evaluated every 30 seconds. Alert triggered if combined_risk > 0.75 (manual override always available via SOS button).
2
Contact resolution
Emergency contacts retrieved from user profile (up to 5). Location formatted as Google Maps link with current timestamp. Risk label and zone name included in message body.
3
Multi-channel dispatch
SMS sent via Twilio API. Email sent via SMTP. Push notification via FCM. All three channels attempted in parallel; first successful delivery counted.
4
Acknowledgement loop
If no ACK received within 60s (user dismissal or contact reply), system re-alerts with escalation. False-alarm cancellation window: 10 seconds after trigger.
